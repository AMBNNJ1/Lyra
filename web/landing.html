Questions & answers

What can Lyra remember?

People, preferences, plans, and important facts you share. You decide what’s saved.

How do I edit or delete memories?

Open the memory view in the app to browse, edit, or wipe items at any time.

Can Lyra start conversations on her own?

Yes. With proactive mode on, she may gently check in during quiet moments.

Does Lyra work offline?

Yes. Local models and local storage are supported; online tools are optional.

How do I turn proactive mode on/off?

Use the Continuous toggle in the chat app or disable proactive checks in settings.
